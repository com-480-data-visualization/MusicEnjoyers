Songs are getting shorter. And it is not a coincidence.

Since TikTok launched in September 2016, the average Billboard Hot 100 has lost more than 10 seconds. Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts followed, rewarding brevity and punishing anything that takes more than three seconds to land the hook.
The attention economy has reshaped pop music.

Genres are shifting.

Pop music no longer belongs to fixed categories. Styles overlap, blend, and evolve faster than ever. What used to be distinct scenes now share the same playlists.
